# Tehara

A side-view puzzle game for kids about the laws of **tumah and taharah**, based on the Mishnah, the Gemara and the Rambam's Sefer Taharah.

It plays like the "pull the pin" / "save the hero" puzzle games: a young Kohen walks through cut-away rooms, fields and roads, and you move roofs, pins, doors, jars and soil so he reaches the goal without becoming tamei. The danger follows the real halacha (Av, Rishon, Sheni, Shlishi; ohel; Beit HaPeras; terumah and kodesh), and every level ends with a short "Why?" card linking to the source on Sefaria.

**Status:** planning. Nothing is built yet.
